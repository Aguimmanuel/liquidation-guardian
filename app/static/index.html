⚡ Enable automatic agent?

In Manual mode (current) every action the guardian proposes waits here for your approval. In Automatic mode the agent can act on your behalf without asking each time:

  • watch your open positions continuously and de-risk any that enter the danger zone — even if you never ask
  • add margin or close positions to protect capital
  • execute the price conditions you arm
  • execute the trades you submit from the Trade panel
⚠️ Note that when using the automatic agent, agents can make mistakes.
Guardrails still apply, but they can't guarantee profit or prevent losses. The guardian never opens new positions on its own — it only executes what you submit or arm. Switch back to Manual at any time.
